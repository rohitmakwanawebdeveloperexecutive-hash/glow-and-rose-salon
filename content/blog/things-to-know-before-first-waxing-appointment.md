# Things to Know Before Your First Waxing Appointment

If you are scheduling your first professional waxing appointment, it is completely normal to feel a bit nervous about the pain level or preparation. The good news? With modern waxing formulations like **Italian Rica Wax** and skilled beauticians, waxing today is faster, gentler, and far more comfortable than you might imagine.

Here is your ultimate beginner's checklist:

---

## 1. Hair Length: The Golden Rule

For wax to effectively grip the hair without breaking it, your hair should be approximately **1/4 inch long** (about the size of a grain of rice). 
- If you've been shaving, stop shaving for at least **2 to 3 weeks** before your appointment.
- If the hair is too short, the wax won't grab cleanly; if it is too long, your beautician will gently trim it before starting.

---

## 2. Exfoliate 24 to 48 Hours Prior

Gently exfoliate the areas you plan to wax using a mild body scrub or loofah 1 to 2 days before your session. This removes dead skin cells surrounding the hair follicles, allowing the wax to pull hair cleanly from the root with less resistance.

---

## 3. Avoid Lotions & Oils on Appointment Day

On the day of your waxing session, shower with a simple non-moisturizing body wash. **Do not apply heavy body lotions, creams, or oils**, as they create a barrier that prevents wax from adhering properly.

---

## 4. Wear Loose, Breathable Cotton Clothing

Friction against freshly waxed skin can cause temporary bumps or irritation. Wear comfortable, loose-fitting cotton clothing (such as palazzos, maxi dresses, or loose t-shirts).

---

## 5. Post-Waxing Care: The First 24 Hours

To keep skin calm and bump-free:
- Avoid hot showers, saunas, and steam baths.
- Skip intense gym workouts that cause excessive sweating.
- Avoid tight synthetic leggings.
- Apply pure aloe vera gel or soothing post-wax tea tree lotion.
