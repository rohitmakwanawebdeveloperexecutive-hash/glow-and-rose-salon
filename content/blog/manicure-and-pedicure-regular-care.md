# Manicure & Pedicure: Why Regular Hand & Foot Care Truly Matters

Our hands and feet work tirelessly every single day. From typing and carrying weights to walking on hard surfaces in heat and dust, our extremities endure significant physical stress and environmental exposure.

Yet, hand and foot care is often postponed until a major party or wedding arrives. Here is why scheduling regular monthly manicures and pedicures is essential for both your wellbeing and confidence:

---

## 1. Prevents Infections & Cracked Heels

Walking barefoot or wearing open footwear exposes feet to urban dust and microbes. Regular pedicures remove dead, hardened skin from heels and gently clean beneath toenails, preventing painful cracked heels, corn buildup, and ingrown nails.

---

## 2. Enhances Blood Circulation

During a luxury manicure or pedicure at **Glow & Rose**, extended reflexology massage stimulates blood flow through your fingers, palms, calves, and feet. This relieves joint stiffness, eases swelling, and distributes natural body heat.

---

## 3. Deep Moisturization & Anti-Aging for Hands

The delicate skin on the back of our hands contains few sebaceous glands and is one of the first areas to display age spots and dryness. Warm rose water soaks followed by vitamin E cuticle oils and hydrating masks replenish lost lipids.

---

## 4. Mental Relaxation & Stress Relief

Taking 45 minutes to submerge your feet in an aroma salt bath or our popular **Crystal Jelly Foot Spa** offers incredible sensory calm. It reduces cortisol levels and leaves you refreshed.
