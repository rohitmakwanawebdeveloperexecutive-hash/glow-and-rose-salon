# The Ultimate Pre-Bridal Beauty Care Timeline & Guide

Your wedding day is one of the most memorable milestones of your life, and feeling radiant and confident is every bride's dream. Flawless bridal makeup starts with a healthy canvas—healthy skin, nourished hair, and well-groomed hands and feet.

Here is your essential month-by-month pre-bridal beauty countdown curated by the bridal makeover specialists at **Glow & Rose Salon & Makeover**:

---

## 3 Months Before the Wedding: The Foundation

- **Skin Consultation:** Schedule a detailed skin analysis to map out treatments for pigmentation, tanning, or acne scars.
- **Hair Rejuvenation:** Begin monthly Keratin or L'Oréal Mythic Hair Spas to repair dryness and split ends.
- **Diet & Hydration:** Incorporate coconut water, seasonal fruits, and leafy greens.

---

## 2 Months Before: Radiance & Consistency

- **Targeted Glow Facials:** Alternate between O3+ Brightening Facials and Hydra-Dew Hyaluronic treatments every 2 weeks.
- **Body Polishing & De-Tan:** Start full body scrubbing and brightening sessions.
- **Hand & Foot Care:** Bi-weekly crystal jelly manicure and pedicure routines.

---

## 1 Month Before: Finalizing Your Bridal Look

- **Bridal Makeup Consultation:** Discuss your outfit colors, jewelry, hairstyle preferences, and drape styles.
- **Avoid Trying New Skincare Products:** Stick strictly to verified formulas to avoid allergic flare-ups.

---

## 3 to 4 Days Before: The Final Touches

- **Full Body Rica Waxing & Threading:** Ensures all redness subsides well before wedding photography.
- **Signature 24K Gold Facial:** Infuses instant radiance and primes the skin for smooth makeup application.
- **Luxury Gel Manicure & Pedicure:** For photo-ready mehndi and ring ceremonies.

---
